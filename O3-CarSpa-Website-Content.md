## 5. Precision Services

*(Upgraded copy — merges the old 8-service list into a premium 3-tier structure, with the full original list folded in beneath as add-ons)*

### Signature Services

**1. Paint Protection Film (PPF)**
Invisible armor for your vehicle's exterior. Self-healing technology that absorbs impacts and prevents rock chips, ensuring a flawless finish under any conditions.

**2. Ceramic Coating**
Molecular-bond protection that redefines gloss. Hydrophobic properties repel water, dirt, and chemical contaminants for years, not months.

**3. Paint Correction**
Micro-abrasive polishing techniques to permanently remove swirl marks, scratches, and oxidation — restoring depth and clarity to absolute perfection.

### Full Service Menu

- **Glass Coating** — Keeps glass surfaces crystal-clear with a highly effective, long-lasting shine.
- **3M Coating** — Protects paint finishes and reduces fading and oxidation over time.
- **Sealant Coating** — Defeats dirt and gives your car's finish a shiny, protected life for years.
- **Exterior Wash** — Complete detail cleaning, washing, and vacuuming that protects the car from the outside in.
- **Intensive Interior Detailing** — Complete deep cleaning for a spotless, shiny, glossy cabin.
- **Car Care Treatment (Würth)** — Ultimate automotive protection; a single application can last up to three years.
- **Polish & Wax** — Polishing revives dimmed paint; waxing seals and protects the freshly revealed coat.
- **Extensive Cleaning** — High-glow foam wash of the entire exterior body plus high-pressure underbody wash — leaving no dirt or dust behind.
